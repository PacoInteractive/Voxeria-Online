Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

Welcome to Voxeria. The protection of your data and providing a safe gaming experience are our top priorities. Below you will find our guidelines regarding data processing.

1. Data Privacy and Storage

We respect your privacy and have designed the game to require as little data as possible:

No Account Required: You do not need a user account to play Voxeria. In particular, no Google account or any other login is required.

No Personal Data: We do not collect, store, or process any of your personal data (such as your real name, address, email, or phone number). We do not require any sensitive information.

Temporary Data & Local Storage: Certain game states or settings are stored strictly locally on your device.

These guidelines are in place to keep Voxeria safe, fair, and fun for all players. Enjoy the game!
